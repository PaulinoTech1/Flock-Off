News Flash FWPD Chief Statement on Flock in Fort Wayne Home Posted on August 31, 2026 Since being appointed Chief of the Fort Wayne Police Department earlier this year, I have been committed to transparency regarding our use of Flock License Plate Reader technology. I want to continue that transparency today by explaining both the decision we have made and how we arrived at it. Following City Council’s vote not to approve the proposed Flock contract, I briefly considered whether a smaller contract could allow the department to retain a limited number of cameras. One of the factors we needed to consider was our obligation under the federal grant that initially funded this program. That issue has now been resolved. The federal government has authorized the Fort Wayne Police Department to close the grant early without penalty or jeopardizing our eligibility for future grant funding. I continue to believe Flock has value as an investigative tool. Our officers and detectives have used this technology to develop investigative leads, locate vehicles associated with criminal activity, recover stolen vehicles, locate missing people, and assist in serious criminal investigations. However, effective policing requires more than technology. It requires the trust and confidence of the community we serve. After careful consideration and discussions with Mayor Tucker, it became clear to me that continuing the Flock program following City Council’s decision carried the risk of damaging that trust. While we may not always agree on the value of a particular law-enforcement tool, the relationship between this department, our elected leaders, and the people of Fort Wayne is far more important than any single piece of technology. For that reason, the decision was made to discontinue the Fort Wayne Police Department’s use of Flock.
