METRO PHOENIX Scottsdale to make Flock camera abuse a crime, as outcry grows Shawn Raymundo Arizona Republic Updated Oct. 8, 2026, 11:20 a.m. MT Amid growing scrutiny over mass surveillance and privacy, Scottsdale is moving to install guardrails around its Flock Safety cameras. In a unanimous vote on Oct. 6, city leaders approved a suite of measures the Scottsdale Police Department believes will “strengthen accountability” of its license plate reader program.
