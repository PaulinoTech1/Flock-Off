Now, Warwick is trying to prevent him from sharing any of the documents from the case’s discovery process — doubling down on stifling his free speech once again. 05/08/2026 Since late 2025, we’ve been keeping tabs on the RI State Police’s work to distribute Flock Safety automated license plate reader (ALPR) cameras across the state. The RISP are installing 39 cameras in 22 municipalities after getting approval from the cities and towns over the past 9 months. In 18 of 22 municipalities, the Chief of Police approved the installation of the RISP-owned camera(s), without the city/town councils hearing the request, much less any public input. Four towns put the request from RISP to a vote, and of those four Warren and Portsmouth rejected the request. Here’s an example of a letter we sent to Warren when the Town Council considered the RISP cameras back in December 2025: https://www.riaclu.org/publications/letter-re-state-police-ask-warren-town-council-to-approve-a-flock-safety-camera/ We have more information available on our website under “Rhode Island State Police Flock Safety Program.” This includes some documents we received through APRA requests, like the RISP’s request letter to cities and towns, and the RISP’s policy governing ALPR camera use.
