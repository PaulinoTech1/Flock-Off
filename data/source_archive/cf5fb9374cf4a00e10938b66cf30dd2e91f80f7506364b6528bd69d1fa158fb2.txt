Networked automated license-plate readers record passing vehicles and let agencies search or share travel records across locations. Candidates and officials disagree over whether to prohibit these networks, keep them with legal limits, or defend their use for investigations. Whitehead moved that Scottsdale draft an ordinance governing automated license plate readers, with proposed criminal penalties for misuse. Her motion also called for independent audits, annual user training, a review of the Flock contract, an evaluation of alternative vendors, a potential transition from Flock and keeping Flock contract funding out of the proposed 2027-28 budget, and the council voted 7-0 for it. The cameras stay in use under added safeguards while a change of vendor is left open.
