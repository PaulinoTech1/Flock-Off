How this statement is classified Mixed or conditional The label describes this statement's response within the context above. Why this label? The reference point is whether networked license-plate reader systems such as Flock should exist and be used. Whitehead moved that Scottsdale draft a license-plate reader ordinance with criminal penalties for misuse, independent audits and training, and review the Flock contract, including a potential transition from Flock and excluding its funding from the fiscal year 2027-28 budget; the council voted 7-0. That keeps the cameras in use under added safeguards while leaving open a change of vendor, so we labeled it Mixed or conditional and placed it at Guardrails, keep using. The closest competing label is Condemned, which would fit a call to end the network now; her motion directs drafting and review. The closest competing remedy is Cut public funding, which the release describes only as potential.
